FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can apply?

Students, researchers, designers, and creators. No prior AI experience needed.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It's free for accepted hackers, meals included.

Do I need a team?

No. Apply solo and meet teammates at team formation on day one.

When do applications open?

Judges: November–December. Hackers: the first week of January 2027.

Where is it held?

In Toronto. Accepted participants get venue and travel details.

How can my company get involved?

Sponsor a challenge, run a workshop, mentor, or judge. Tap "Partner with us".
