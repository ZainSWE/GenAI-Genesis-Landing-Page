About GenAI Genesis

Canada's Largest AI Hackathon.

GenAI Genesis is a 36-hour AI hackathon taking place in March 2027 at the University of Toronto in downtown Toronto.

Over an intense weekend of building and learning, you’ll collaborate with top mentors, engineers, and industry leaders to build working prototypes, take part in hands-on workshops, and present your projects to judges.

We exist to empower the next generation of builders to use AI collaboratively, responsibly, and at scale. AI is changing how the world works. We want students to have a hand in deciding what comes next.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can participate?

We welcome applicants from Canada and around the world, at all skill levels. If you’re curious about AI and excited to build something, we’d love to hear from you.

Do I need AI or programming experience?

No prior AI experience is required! Some programming knowledge can help, but it’s not a requirement. You’ll have access to resources and mentors to help you along the way.

Do I need a team before applying?

Nope! Everyone applies individually, and we’ll help you find teammates. If you already have a team in mind, make sure each member submits their own application.

How many people can be on a team?

Teams can have up to four people, from any school, country, or experience level. Just make sure everyone is participating in the same format, in-person hackers team up with other in-person hackers, and virtual hackers team up with other virtual hackers.

Is the hackathon in person or online?

Both! You can join us in person at the University of Toronto’s St. George campus or participate online. Please choose one format when applying, we don’t accept applications for both.

When is the hackathon?

GenAI Genesis returns in March 2027! Follow us on Instagram for the exact dates and schedule.

How long is the hackathon?

You’ll have 36 hours to work on your project. Plan to stick around afterwards for the closing ceremony and awards!

When do hacker applications open?

Follow us on Instagram to hear when applications open. If you have any questions in the meantime, reach out at contact@genaigenesis.ca.

How do I apply?

Once applications open, you’ll find the application form right here on our website.

How much does it cost?

It’s completely free to participate! We’ll also provide resources and mentorship to support you throughout the weekend.

Is acceptance guaranteed?

We review every application, but submitting one doesn’t guarantee a spot. You don’t need an impressive AI resume to apply, we welcome beginners, too.
